**JavelinMasters** is a precision sports game that simulates the ancient Olympic discipline of javelin throwing. Hone your run‑up, angle, and release timing to launch the javelin as far as possible. Compete against AI opponents or aim for a personal best.

> *Run, plant, throw – become a legend of the field.*

---

## 📖 Overview

JavelinMasters captures the core mechanics of real javelin throwing in an accessible arcade style. You control the athlete’s speed, the javelin’s angle, and the release point. Wind conditions, stamina, and technique all affect the final distance. The game includes a career mode, quick play, and multiplayer (hot‑seat) options.

Current features (subject to change as development continues):
- Three‑phase throwing system: approach run, plant, release
- Realistic physics simulation (drag, gravity, lift)
- Wind direction and strength variation per throw
- Progression system with upgrades (technique, strength, equipment)
- Leaderboards and achievements

---

## ✨ Features

- **Authentic throwing mechanics** – Timing bars or analog input for run speed, angle, and release.
- **Multiple game modes** – Quick throw, tournament, training, and online leaderboards.
- **Customisation** – Unlock different javelins, skins, and stadiums.
- **Visual feedback** – Trajectory prediction, replay camera, and distance markers.
- **Mobile & desktop ready** – Works with touch, mouse, or keyboard.
